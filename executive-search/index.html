Senior leadership asks a great deal of one role.

A company's senior leaders set direction, build and keep strong teams, and answer to owners, boards, employees and customers, often through periods of growth or change. The role calls for business judgment, financial understanding and the ability to lead people.

When one hire carries that much, the search deserves the same care.
